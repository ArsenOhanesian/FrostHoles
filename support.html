Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?
Tasks are saved locally on your iPhone. There is no account required for the planner features described in the App.

How do I delete all tasks?
Open FrostHoles → Settings → Data → Reset all tasks. This cannot be undone.

The Today screen looks wrong or tasks don’t update
Try force-quitting and reopening the App. If problems continue, reset tasks (above) or reinstall FrostHoles (you will lose local tasks unless you back up your device).

Notifications
Push notifications are optional. You can turn them off in iOS Settings → Notifications → FrostHoles.

Tracking permission
If you see a tracking prompt, you can decline and still use the task planner. See our Privacy Policy.

When contacting support, please include

  • Device model (e.g. iPhone 15)
  • iOS version (Settings → General → About)
  • FrostHoles version (Settings → About in the App)
  • Short description of the issue and steps to reproduce
