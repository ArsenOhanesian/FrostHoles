This Privacy Policy describes how YOUR_COMPANY (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use the FrostHoles mobile application (“App”).

Summary

  • Your tasks and notes are stored on your device unless you delete the app or reset data in Settings.
  • We do not require an account to use the core task planner.
  • The App may use third-party services for analytics, attribution, and notifications (see below).

Information we process

Task data (local). Titles, notes, categories, priorities, due dates, and completion status are saved locally on your device (e.g. via app storage). We do not operate a FrostHoles cloud sync server for tasks described in this policy unless we tell you otherwise in the App.

Device and usage data. Depending on your settings and permissions, the App or our partners may collect or receive:

  • Device identifiers and advertising identifiers (e.g. if you allow App Tracking Transparency)
  • App install and campaign attribution data
  • Push notification tokens (if you allow notifications)
  • Technical data such as IP address, app version, and general usage events

Third-party services

We may use SDKs and services including, but not limited to:

  • Meta (Facebook) SDK — analytics and advertising-related features as configured in the App.
  • AppsFlyer — install attribution and marketing measurement.
  • Apple Push Notification service (APNs) — to deliver notifications if enabled.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review:

  • Meta Privacy Policy
  • AppsFlyer Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

Permissions

  • Tracking (ATT). If prompted, you can allow or deny tracking used for advertising measurement. Denying does not block basic task features.
  • Notifications. Optional. Used only if you enable push notifications.

Network requests

The App may communicate with our or partners’ servers (for example, configuration, attribution, or push-related endpoints). Data sent depends on App version and your choices.

Retention and deletion

  • Local task data remains until you delete tasks, use “Reset all tasks” in Settings, or uninstall the App.
  • Partner retention is governed by their policies.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
